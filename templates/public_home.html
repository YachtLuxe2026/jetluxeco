About

Broward-based. Independent. Compliant.

JETLUXECO is a licensed indirect air carrier and charter broker operating under 14 CFR Part 295, based in Broward County, Florida. Sister brand to YACHTLUXE.

We don't own aircraft. We don't sell memberships. What we do is negotiate every leg against every available operator, using real-time inventory feeds and direct operator relationships, so you fly for what the market actually costs — not what an app decides to show you.
